01 — About

Code is just the tool. People are what matter.

I’m Gustavo, a front-end developer in Angra dos Reis, Brazil. I believe no interface is good unless the people using it can feel it — and that every detail on a screen needs a reason to be there.

I have systems in production for the City of Angra dos Reis and take on independent projects. I’m expanding into the back end with PHP/Laravel and C#/.NET so I can deliver complete solutions.

When I’m not coding, I’m listening to music, hunting for design references, or trying to figure out why that CSS didn’t do what I wanted. Comes with the job.

  1. Empathy first

    I think about who will use it before writing the first line.

  2. Details matter

    The millimeters and milliseconds that make the difference.

  3. Mobile first

    If it doesn’t work on a phone, it doesn’t work.

  4. Always learning
